---
title: Player Guides
description: Guides for players who want to use Tsuchi mods.
---

Before you start, you'll need Pikmin 4 version 1.1.1 or 2.0.0, or Pikmin 4 Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, and either an emulator
that can load mods or a console that runs Atmosphere. Start with
[Install Tsuchi](/players/install-tsuchi/) for an emulator, or
[Install on a console](/players/install-console/) for Atmosphere.

After you [install a mod](/players/install-a-mod/), open **Settings > Mods** in the
game to enable or disable it, find out why a mod is blocked, and set your load order.
Dependencies start first, and your order decides the rest. See
[Manage mods](/players/mod-list/).

If you want to create a mod instead, check out the [Developer Guides](/develop/).
